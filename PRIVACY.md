# Privacy policy — projects-backup

projects-backup is a personal command-line backup tool used only by its author
(Deniz Erdogan, erdogan3333333@gmail.com). It uploads encrypted archives to the
author's own Google Drive through the Drive API.

- The app reads and writes files only in the author's own Google Drive account.
- It collects, stores and shares no data about any other person.
- No data is sent to third parties or to any server other than Google's.
- Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Last updated: 19 October 2026.
